HOW TO READ MARKET INTELLIGENCE

Use each module as a separate evidence lens. Read the status first, then the definition below; combine modules before forming a market view.

CAPITAL FLOWPOSITIVE · NEUTRAL · NEGATIVE

Tracks Bitcoin ETF capital entering or leaving the market. Sustained positive flow supports institutional demand; negative flow signals net withdrawal.

LIQUIDITYEXPANDING · STABLE · CONTRACTING

Tracks stablecoin purchasing power. Expansion means crypto-native liquidity is growing; contraction means it is shrinking.

DERIVATIVESNEUTRAL · ACTIVE · CROWDED · DELEVERAGING

Combines funding and open interest. ACTIVE means leverage is expanding without our crowding threshold; CROWDED flags stronger one-sided leverage; DELEVERAGING means open interest is contracting sharply.

MARKET STRESSLOW · NORMAL · ELEVATED · EXTREME

Combines realized volatility, unusual volume and drawdown. Higher stress means market conditions are becoming more disorderly, not automatically bearish.

ON-CHAIN ACTIVITYEXPANDING · STABLE · CONTRACTING

Compares active addresses, transaction count and hash rate with recent baselines. EXPANDING confirms stronger network participation; CONTRACTING indicates weakening activity. If price rises while on-chain activity contracts, treat the divergence as something to monitor—not an automatic sell signal.

COMBINATION RULE

Interpret modules together. A single favorable status is not a buy signal. DATA PENDING means a source is unavailable or not reliable enough; no placeholder score is substituted.
