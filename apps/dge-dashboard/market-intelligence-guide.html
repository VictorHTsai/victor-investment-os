How to Read BTC Market Intelligence

Evidence Layer · Victor Investment OS

Purpose

BTC Market Intelligence is an evidence layer, not a Buy/Sell model. It organizes five independent lenses so you can see whether capital, liquidity, leverage, stress and network activity are confirming or contradicting one another.

Read status first → inspect the underlying metrics → compare the time context → combine modules. Never interpret one module in isolation.

1 · Capital Flow

What it measures: net capital entering or leaving U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs. POSITIVE indicates sustained net inflow; NEUTRAL mixed/insufficient directional flow; NEGATIVE sustained net outflow.

Positive flow is supportive evidence, not proof that price must rise. Price response to continued flow also matters.

2 · Liquidity

What it measures: stablecoin supply and its rate of change as a proxy for crypto-native purchasing power. EXPANDING means the liquidity base is growing; STABLE means no strong change; CONTRACTING means it is shrinking.

7D / 30D / 90D Supply Change provide different horizons. 30D Acceleration asks whether liquidity growth itself is speeding up or slowing down.

Stablecoin supply is a liquidity proxy; it does not mean all newly issued stablecoins will buy BTC.

3 · Derivatives Positioning

What it measures: leverage using funding, open interest and BTC price context. NEUTRAL means leverage is not unusually stretched; ACTIVE means leverage is expanding below the crowding rule; CROWDED flags stronger one-sided leverage; DELEVERAGING means open interest is contracting sharply.

Funding Rate is the periodic payment between perpetual-futures longs and shorts. Open Interest (OI) is outstanding derivatives exposure. Rising OI means leverage participation is expanding; falling OI means positions are being closed or liquidated.

High OI is not automatically bearish. Direction, funding, price response and speed of change matter.

4 · Market Stress

What it measures: disorder in trading conditions using realized volatility, unusual volume and drawdown. LOW / NORMAL / ELEVATED / EXTREME describe intensity, not direction.

Realized Volatility measures actual price movement. Volume Ratio compares recent activity with its baseline. Drawdown measures decline from a recent high.

EXTREME does not mean “sell.” Stress can accompany capitulation, deleveraging, breakout or regime transition.

5 · On-Chain Activity

What it measures: Bitcoin network participation and infrastructure using public Coin Metrics Community data. EXPANDING means multiple activity measures strengthen; STABLE means mixed/modest evidence; CONTRACTING means multiple measures weaken.

Active Addresses approximates on-chain participation. Transaction Count measures transaction activity. Hash Rate reflects computational infrastructure committed to mining.

This is On-Chain Activity, not whale ownership or Supply Concentration. Supply Concentration remains a separate DGE factor.

6 · Status & Data Terminology

DATA PENDING means a source is unavailable, incomplete or not reliable enough; no substitute value is fabricated. Percentile ranks the current observation against its stated historical window; it is not a probability of future return. 7D / 30D / 90D are lookback horizons.

7 · Combining the Modules

Look for confluence and divergence. Capital Flow POSITIVE + Liquidity EXPANDING + On-Chain EXPANDING is broader confirmation than one favorable module. Strong inflows alongside CROWDED derivatives and ELEVATED stress describe a different risk structure.

Market Intelligence intentionally preserves the individual modules so conflicting evidence remains visible.

8 · Relationship to Market State Monitor

Market Intelligence asks “What evidence is present?” Selected validated metrics feed DGE factors such as External Capital Flow and Derivatives Positioning. The Market State Monitor then asks “What state best explains the combined evidence?”

9 · Recommended Reading Order

Module Status → Key Metrics → Change over time → Historical context → Cross-module confirmation/divergence → Market State Monitor → Decision Context.

A status is evidence, not a trading instruction.
