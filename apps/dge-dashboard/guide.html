How to Read the BTC Market State Monitor

Dynamic Game Engine · DGE V1

Purpose

The monitor does not simply predict whether BTC will rise or fall. It asks: What market state best fits the current evidence? What should happen next if that hypothesis is correct? What evidence would challenge it?

1 · Six Market States

Accumulation — evidence consistent with supply absorption or position building.

Market Test — pressure is testing how price and flow respond.

Shakeout — strong selling/clearing pressure; watch whether marginal selling impact begins to weaken.

Markup — upward price expansion is becoming dominant.

Distribution — supply pressure rises and incremental capital may move price less efficiently.

Neutral / Unclear — evidence is insufficient or the market may be transitioning.

A state is a market-structure hypothesis, not a Buy/Sell signal.

2 · Leading State Probability & State Clarity

SHAKEOUT · 24% means Shakeout has the highest relative support among the six states. It does not mean BTC has a 24% probability of falling, and it is not a trade win rate.

State Clarity summarizes how separated the leading hypothesis is from competing states. Low clarity means the evidence remains mixed.

3 · Nine Evidence Factors

R · Market Regime
Trend and broad market structure.

L · Liquidity
Trading and liquidity environment.

F · External Capital Flow
ETF, stablecoin and other external flows.

C · Supply Concentration
Proxy for concentration of effectively tradable supply.

P · Price Position
Where price sits inside recent structure.

V · Market Response
How price responds to comparable buying/selling pressure.

D · Derivatives Positioning
Open interest, funding and liquidations.

E · Event / Shock Risk
Tail-risk override for scheduled macro events and realized BTC market shocks. 0 means no material shock condition is currently detected; higher values mean the current market-state hypothesis deserves progressively more caution. In V1 the score is intentionally tiered rather than continuous: roughly 0–34 = LOW/NORMAL, 35–64 = WATCH, 65–74 = ELEVATED, and 75–100 = HIGH/EXTREME. At E ≥ 75, DGE deliberately reduces state certainty and shifts weight toward Neutral / Unclear because a shock may invalidate the prior regime. E is not the probability that BTC will fall and is not directional by itself.

T · Cycle / Time Context
Timing and cycle context for the current state.

Scores are standardized evidence measures. 0–100 is not a return forecast and higher is not automatically bullish.

4 · Data Status

LIVE means real data currently drives the factor. DATA PENDING means the source has not yet been connected and the value should not be interpreted. A future STALE label will identify data older than its freshness threshold.

5 · What Should Happen Next?

DGE states the observation expected if its leading hypothesis is correct. New evidence can then confirm or weaken that hypothesis.

6 · What Would Challenge This View?

Every hypothesis must be falsifiable. An invalidation condition is evidence that should reduce the weight of the current state rather than trigger a retrospective rewrite of the story.

7 · Decision Log

Model output and human trading decisions are stored separately. This allows later evaluation of both model quality and execution quality.

8 · Walk-Forward Audit

Important snapshots are frozen and evaluated after 7, 14 and 30 days using future return, MFE, MAE, calibration/Brier metrics and comparison with the Compression V1 baseline.

9 · Recommended Reading Order

Leading State → State Clarity → State Probabilities → Evidence Factors → Expected Observation → Invalidation → Wait for new evidence → Trading decision.

Do not trade solely because of a state label. DGE organizes evidence and creates testable market-state hypotheses; it does not replace risk management or human judgment.

Methodology Note

“Dynamic Game Engine” is the name of the Victor Investment OS methodology. The user-facing term “Market State Monitor” is deliberately descriptive: the engine continuously evaluates competing market-state hypotheses and updates them as market evidence changes. It does not claim knowledge of any specific dealer's private inventory or intent.
